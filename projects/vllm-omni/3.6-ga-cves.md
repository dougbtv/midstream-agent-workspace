## Method notes

- The immutable digest above is a multi-architecture image index. Dispatching
  Clair directly against that index returned `status: unsupported` in run
  `45428587289`; dispatching the release tag with `platform=linux/amd64`
  correctly resolved and scanned the child manifest.
- Advisory aliases were normalized through OSV so duplicate GHSA/PYSEC records
  appear once under their canonical CVE ID.
- Trivy vuln-mode cross-check run
  [`45428589859`](https://github.com/neuralmagic/nm-cicd/actions/runs/45428589859)
  completed successfully against the same linux/amd64 RHEL 9.8 image.
  It reported 50 CVE IDs plus two GHSA IDs (26 High, 24 Medium, 2 Low before
  alias normalization). Thirteen CVEs overlap the Clair list; three are
  Clair-only; and 37 are Trivy-only.
- The Trivy-only set contains 21 High and 16 Medium findings: 32 RPM findings
  (17 High / 15 Medium) and five Python findings (4 High / 1 Medium). This
  broader cross-check does not replace the documented Clair sign-off list,
  but its supplemental High findings should be reconciled with Product
  Security before describing the image as clean across both scanners.
