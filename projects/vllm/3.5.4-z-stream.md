# RHAIIS 3.5.4 z-stream

## Purpose and current state

Doug is the Midstream z-stream rotation owner for RHAIIS 3.5.4. This is the
durable working note for the release; it is intentionally an initial map, not
a runbook.

**Important:** the operational workflow has not yet been learned or validated.
Before taking release actions, determine the actual z-stream procedure,
ownership boundaries, required approvals, source/release branches, pipeline
entry points, and evidence expected at each gate. Do not infer those details
from prior release tickets.

## Discovery checklist

1. Get a walkthrough from the prior z-stream owner or release engineer and
   identify the authoritative checklist (the epic references a Midstream
   validation-plan spreadsheet and a Miro process board).
2. Establish the exact release branches/tags and the first decision due before
   the 2026-11-02 planning freeze.
3. Identify who owns each gate: CVE triage and backports, wheel validation,
   AIPCC builds, model validation, AMD validation, and the final AIPCC CI/CD
   production trigger.
4. Locate the `nm-cicd` workflows named by the stories: Snyk image security
   scan, accept-sync, image validation, and model-validation-ocp.
5. Record expected artifacts, links, approvers, and results per hardware lane
   here as the process becomes known.
